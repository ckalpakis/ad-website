Before you book

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Want to talk about your specific setup?

Ask Carson directly
Do you just generate leads?

Lead generation is the first step. The system also covers follow-up, qualification, nurture, and routing qualified prospects toward appointments. Your team handles estimates and closing.

Do I need a new CRM?

Not necessarily. We review your current setup first and determine what can connect to it. If it is part of the problem, we discuss what needs to change.

How does the AI fit in?

AI supports speed and consistency in follow-up and qualification. SMS and voice workflows can respond to new inquiries and keep the conversation moving. The setup depends on your business and process.

What will it cost, including ad spend?

We need to understand your market, offer, and current setup before quoting. On the strategy call, ask about both the system’s cost and the separate advertising budget so you can assess the total investment.

Do you guarantee leads or revenue?

No. Results depend on your offer, market, budget, follow-up, sales process, and fulfillment. The screenshots show selected past outcomes, not a guarantee of what your business will achieve.

What happens on the strategy call?

We discuss your service, capacity, current lead flow, and where prospects are dropping off. From there, we can talk about whether this system fits your business and what a next step would involve.
